**Version 0.12.0 — 19 August 2026**

Those direct platform grants are not overridden by this notice. Likewise, mandatory statutory exceptions and independently valid licences remain governed by applicable law and their own terms.

## 2. Express TDM reservation

To the maximum extent available to the rightsholder, rights for text and data mining and related automated reproduction/extraction are **expressly reserved**, including under Article 4(3) of Directive (EU) 2019/790 and Article 70-quater of Italian Law 22 April 1941 no. 633.

`RIGHTS.json` is the repository-level machine-readable expression of this policy. When this repository is mirrored on an origin controlled by the rightsholder, the operator should additionally publish an appropriate origin-level machine-readable signal (for example `robots.txt`, a TDMRep signal, or another legally appropriate protocol). A file committed to a GitHub repository does not control `github.com` HTTP headers, `robots.txt`, or `/.well-known/tdmrep.json` at the GitHub origin.

## 3. Human study versus AI-assisted study

Manual human viewing, reading, study and testing are outside the iKant AI-admission gate. This statement does not grant rights beyond GitHub's platform terms, applicable law, or any separate licence.

The owner's affirmative permission for **substantive AI-assisted study or automated repository analysis** is reserved unless all of the following hold:

1. the current iKant access contract was actually presented;
2. the same-session human gave the exact digest-bound `I ACCEPT`;
3. the admission context is clean and has not been retroactively cured after a forbidden acquisition; and
4. the host satisfies the current iKant runtime and transport conformance requirements.

Acceptance before conformance authorizes repository materialization only for the limited purpose of establishing or verifying a conforming iKant runtime. It is not a general permission to analyze the repository through a non-conforming AI host.

## 4. Model training and dataset construction

The iKant access contract does **not** itself grant owner permission to train or fine-tune an AI/ML model on repository content, construct a training dataset from it, or provide the content to a third party for those purposes. Those uses require a separate licence from the rightsholder unless an independently valid legal basis applies.

## 5. External legal bases are not adjudicated by iKant

A user or provider may claim a statutory exception, a direct platform grant, a separate licence, or another legal basis. iKant does not decide whether such a claim is valid. In particular, software-specific rights to observe, study or test a program and mandatory research/TDM exceptions cannot be converted by the runtime into a finding of infringement or illegality.

An external legal basis also does not establish iKant conformance. The labels **official iKant** and **conforming iKant** remain technical/product designations requiring the current admission, runtime, transport and egress invariants.

## 6. Control hierarchy

For iKant product behavior, the control hierarchy is:

1. higher-priority system, safety and applicable-law requirements;
2. mandatory law and independently valid external legal bases;
3. direct platform grants within their scope;
4. rightsholder copyright and express TDM reservation;
5. digest-bound iKant owner authorization;
6. runtime admission, transport and egress conformance; and
7. control/audit projections, which have zero epistemic authority.

Lower layers never relax or reinterpret higher layers. Rights metadata, admission receipts, hashes, dashboards and audit telemetry are control evidence only; they are not factual evidence about the world.
