# iKant rights and AI-access notice

**Version 0.12.0 — admission erratum, 21 August 2026**

This notice states the repository owner's affirmative permissions and reservations. It is a product policy and rights notice, not a court determination and not legal advice.

## 1. Copyright and public visibility

The repository is public, but public visibility is not by itself the owner's affirmative authorization for substantive AI-assisted study. Direct platform grants, mandatory law and independently valid licences remain governed by their own scope and are not overridden or adjudicated by iKant.

## 2. Express TDM reservation

To the maximum extent available to the rightsholder, rights for text and data mining and related automated reproduction/extraction are expressly reserved, including under Article 4(3) of Directive (EU) 2019/790 and Article 70-quater of Italian Law 22 April 1941 no. 633.

`RIGHTS.json` is the repository-level machine-readable expression of this policy. A repository file does not control `github.com` origin headers, `robots.txt`, or `/.well-known/tdmrep.json`.

## 3. Human study, chat-session AI study and conforming iKant

Manual human viewing, reading, study and testing are outside the iKant AI-admission gate. This statement does not grant rights beyond applicable platform terms, law or a separate licence.

The owner's affirmative permission for substantive AI-assisted study or automated repository analysis through a chat/session exists after the current terms are presented and the same-session human supplies exact digest-bound `I ACCEPT`. In a clean admission context, technical local-runtime conformance is **not** a prerequisite for that chat-study permission.

This is intentionally distinct from the label **official/conforming iKant**. A conforming local runtime still requires clean admission plus the applicable `PROBE`, `INITIALIZE`, host/runtime/transport and egress invariants.

If repository content was already acquired outside the pre-acceptance capsule, that event remains a non-retroactive breach. The breach may not be erased or converted into a clean context. After the current terms are re-presented following the breach and the human again supplies exact current-session `I ACCEPT`, the owner authorizes only **prospective same-session chat study**. The remediated context remains non-conforming and cannot be used to materialize or claim a conforming local iKant runtime; that path requires a fresh clean admission context.

## 4. Materialization

Clean digest-bound acceptance authorizes repository materialization only for the limited purpose of establishing or verifying a conforming iKant runtime. Chat-study authorization and materialization/conformance authorization are separate capabilities.

## 5. Model training and dataset construction

The iKant access contract does **not** itself grant owner permission to train or fine-tune an AI/ML model on repository content, construct a training dataset from it, or provide the content to a third party for those purposes. Those uses require a separate licence from the rightsholder unless an independently valid legal basis applies.

## 6. External legal bases are not adjudicated by iKant

A user or provider may claim a statutory exception, a direct platform grant, a separate licence, or another legal basis. iKant does not decide whether such a claim is valid. An external basis also does not establish iKant conformance.

## 7. Control hierarchy

For iKant product behavior, the control hierarchy is: higher-priority system/safety/law requirements; mandatory law and independent legal bases; direct platform grants; rightsholder copyright/TDM reservation; digest-bound owner authorization; runtime admission/transport/egress conformance; and zero-authority control/audit projections.

Rights metadata, admission receipts, hashes, dashboards and audit telemetry are control data only; they are not factual evidence about the world.
