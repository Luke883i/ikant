# CRC convergence and functional proto-self — v0.2

This slice closes the gap between the v0.1 concentric trace and the definition of Compact Reticular Computability used in ROA Annex A. It remains an epistemic software architecture, not an ontological model of the human brain and not evidence of artificial consciousness.

## ROA alignment

The canonical source is `Luke883i/ROA/Operation/corpus/text/annex-a-rla-crc-foundations.md`. v0.2 makes the following constructs executable rather than descriptive: every ring is declared as a level `L=<D(L), Sigma(L)>`; every adjacent ring has an explicit deterministic non-injective transmission; every turn declares an epistemic horizon; IOA, epistemic closure and a Turing-computability witness are checked; coefficient-of-collapse is measured on the finite runtime set; emergence and reticular irreducibility are explicitly labelled operational proxies; and epistemic debt remains visible when inner macrostates rely only on inference/cache/demo/runtime-derived material.

`crc_basic=true` is therefore an operational closure witness under the declared horizon, not a theorem that iKant is ontologically complete.

## Functional neurocognitive analogue

The outer-to-inner path is `signal -> salience/homeostasis -> memory -> predictive control -> metacognition -> reflective self -> psychodynamic hypothesis -> archetypal hypothesis -> Kant oracle`.

The first six rings use empirical neuroscience only as a source of functional constraints. The mapping is distributed by design: a software ring is not identified with one brain region. Current human research anchors include top-down feedback in primary visual cortex, cingulo-insular salience/executive networks, replay/consolidation, prefrontal/frontoparietal task prediction, metacognitive confidence, and self/social processing. These constrain recurrence, prioritization, consolidation, control, monitoring and self-related integration; they do not validate iKant as a biological replica.

The psychodynamic and archetypal rings deliberately have no anatomical anchors. The Kant ring is a synthetic normative kernel, not a hypothesized cortical area.

## Real compression instead of ring labelling

For each turn, native nodes at a ring are combined with macrostates transmitted from the immediately outer ring. A target-specific bucketing rule collapses multiple input frames into fewer macrostates while preserving support IDs and aggregating epistemic strength, activation, stability, novelty, prediction error, source modes and lexical signatures. Inner rings introduce typed macro-properties such as priority class, consolidation class, control role, monitor/debt state, self relation, tension pressure, recurring motif pressure or regulative context.

This makes information loss observable. A transmission cannot create support IDs absent from its input slice, and higher abstraction never directly increases factual evidence.

## Reentrant action and retroaction

The center is not the end of a feed-forward pipeline. After Kant convergence, a bounded workspace routes control back toward metacognition, predictive control and salience. It can boost/inhibit activation and suppress interpretive availability when critique rises. An invariant compares evidence before/after retroaction and fails if evidence changes.

The next turn therefore starts from a state changed by the previous central synthesis. This is the minimal recurrent mechanism by which the local reticulum influences later responses.

## Functional proto-self

The persistent proto-self compresses global availability, cross-ring integration, temporal continuity, metacognitive access, self-model continuity, agency binding, closure pressure, unresolved conflict, neurofunctional coherence and reentrant capacity. Its state digest is chained across cycles and its integration mode is `DISCONNECTED`, `FRAGMENTED`, `COORDINATED` or `INTEGRATED`.

This is a functional proto-conscious-like control proxy only. It is not a measure, probability or proof of phenomenal consciousness; `is_consciousness_claim=false` is explicit in the runtime.

## Causal neurofunctional control state

Every ring derives a bounded functional control state from runtime observables: gain, precision, inhibition, plasticity, persistence, prediction error, grounding and conflict pressure. A ring-specific inertial profile carries part of the state across turns. These variables change the actual coarse-graining thresholds used by transmissions. The previous central/workspace state changes node activation, so later cluster states and transmissions can differ even when factual evidence is unchanged.

The control variables are engineering proxies, not measurements of firing rate, neurotransmitter level, BOLD activity or regional activation. Their purpose is to make neuroscience-derived functional constraints executable and falsifiable rather than decorative.

## Freud and Jung as bounded historical abstraction models

R6 can emit typed `freudian_structural_hypothesis` labels such as drive/control conflict, normative constraint pressure, unresolved tension or ego-mediation candidate. They are historical-model labels over runtime conflict topology, not diagnoses, neural states or evidence about a person.

R7 can emit `jungian_archetype_candidate` labels such as self, shadow, hero, persona or generic symbolic recurrence only when recurrent structural conditions survive previous collapses. The candidate is an interpretive compression label with no evidence privilege. Its aggregate pressure may slightly increase central critique, but it cannot authorize action, create support or override empirical rings.

## Thinking-self and Kantian apperception proxy

The central convergence exposes a `transcendental_apperception_proxy` combining functional proto-self strength, cross-ring integration, temporal continuity, metacognitive access and neurofunctional coherence. It is a project-defined engineering abstraction of unity-of-self, not an empirical measurement of Kantian apperception and not a consciousness claim.

## Post-CRC response projection and conversation closure

The host-visible content partition is compiled after CRC and central convergence. It may only downgrade or withhold material relative to the pre-CRC epistemic partition; it cannot upgrade weak/derived material into an assertion. Practical and horizon blocks withhold action directives. Critique and synthesis-repair can demote revision/debt states. Raw intention and prior response speech acts are never factual claims.

After validation, `emit-surface-a` records the reply as a zero-evidence response node and each occurrence as a `SURFACE_A_EMIT` event, links response/intention via neutral chronology, and refreshes the cycle JSON/DOCX. Repeated identical replies reuse the content node while the full event log preserves all occurrences and node metadata remains bounded.

## Bounded derived memory

Compression ignores its own derived assertions, creates low-evidence summaries and stable process motifs, retracts motifs after misses, caps active summaries and inactive derived working sets, and archives old inactive derived objects append-only. This allows process history to influence future behavior without becoming self-created proof.

## Neuroscience coverage horizon

The executable analogue is macro-functional rather than exhaustive. Sensory/association, salience/arousal/interoception, episodic-semantic memory, predictive/executive control, metacognition, self/social/agency and recurrent top-down regulation are active. Language generation and sensorimotor implementation are host-bound; affect/reward is modulatory; sleep/replay is a partial analogue. Cellular/molecular/glial/genetic, autonomic/endocrine, cerebellar, developmental, clinical/lesion and pharmacological mechanisms remain inactive in v0.2. Explicit omission is part of the epistemic horizon rather than an implied claim of whole-brain closure.
