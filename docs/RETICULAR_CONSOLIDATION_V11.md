8. **CI compression:** repository-wide CI + one reticular boundary workflow are sufficient; historical version-specific release workflows are removed after their checks are absorbed.

10. **Claim boundary:** no consciousness/brain-equivalence claim; no private chain-of-thought persistence; telemetry never promoted to evidence.
